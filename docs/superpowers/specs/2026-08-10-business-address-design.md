# Business Address Design

## Goal

Add Mentor Advisors Equity Group LLC's physical business address to the public website so the business identity and contact details are clearly visible for SimpleTexting A2P verification.

## Approved content

Mentor Advisors Equity Group LLC  
545 NE 141st St  
North Miami, FL 33161-3128

## Placement

The address will appear in two existing compliance-visible locations:

1. The site footer's Contact Us section.
2. The Privacy Policy's Contact Us section, alongside the existing email address and phone number.

## Presentation

The existing page structure and visual styling will be preserved. The address will use semantic address markup and line breaks so it remains readable on desktop and mobile. No form behavior, consent language, navigation, or other business information will change.

## Verification

An automated test will require the complete legal name and postal address to appear in both approved locations. The full test suite will run before publication, followed by a check of the deployed public page.
