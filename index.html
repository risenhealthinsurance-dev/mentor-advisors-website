Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Mentor Advisors Equity Group LLC ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you interact with our website and agent communication programs.

2. Information Collection

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide through our website forms, including:

  • Full Name
  • Mobile Phone Number
  • Email Address

3. Use of Information

Your information is used exclusively for business operations relating to our agent network, including:

  • Providing agent training and resource updates
  • Sending business-development announcements
  • Appointment and event reminders
  • Compliance and operational account notifications

4. Data Sharing & Privacy

We do not sell, rent, or lease our contact lists to third parties. Information collected through our SMS program will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes. Data is only shared with internal operations staff and authorized service providers required to deliver our communications.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to opt-out of our communications at any time. For SMS, reply STOP to any message. For email, use the unsubscribe link provided in the message. You may also contact us to request access to or deletion of your personal data.

SMS Terms & Conditions

Program Description

Mentor Advisors Equity Group LLC offers an SMS communication program for its affiliated agent network. By opting in, you agree to receive text messages regarding agent onboarding, training materials, agency updates, appointment reminders, and business-development opportunities.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on agency activity and individual agent needs. You can expect to receive periodically scheduled updates and reminders.

STOP & HELP Commands

You can cancel the SMS service at any time. Just text "STOP" to the short code or phone number you received the message from. After you send the SMS message "STOP" to us, we will send you an SMS message to confirm that you have been unsubscribed. After this, you will no longer receive SMS messages from us. If you want to join again, just sign up as you did the first time and we will start sending SMS messages to you again.

If you are experiencing issues with the messaging program you can reply with the keyword HELP for more assistance, or you can get help directly at admin@mentoradvisors.org.

Cost and Carriers

Message and data rates may apply for any messages sent to you from us and to us from you. If you have any questions about your text plan or data plan, it is best to contact your wireless provider. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Consent Requirement

Consent to receive marketing or informational text messages is not required as a condition of purchasing any goods or services or entering into any professional agreement with Mentor Advisors Equity Group LLC.
